# Witcher 3 VR – Remastered fork, v0.9.8v2-remastered.3

First regular (non-pre-release) release of this fork of
[Witcher 3 VR](https://github.com/tig3rmast3r/witcher3-vr) by tig3rmast3r. All credit for the mod
goes to him; this fork adds support for The Witcher 3 Remastered on top of his v0.9.8v2 release.
Many of the 5.x improvements come from
[kaarele/witcher3-remastered-vr](https://github.com/kaarele/witcher3-remastered-vr) (MIT), taken
over feature by feature.

**On 5.x, use this fork only.** The original v0.9.8v2 is made for 4.04; on 5.x its scripts fail to
compile (`@wrapMethod cannot wrap 'HorseRiding::OnGameCameraPostTick'`) and its game hooks do not
match.

## Game versions

- **The Witcher 3 Remastered, patch 5.01** (GOG): Stereo or Mono with the game's FXAA, or with
  **FSR per eye** (launcher switch). TAAU, ray tracing, AER + AFW, OptiScaler, ReShade and OFXR are
  not ported to 5.x yet.
- **The Witcher 3 Next-Gen 4.04**: as in the original v0.9.8v2, plus the fixes of this fork.

The 5.x port is made for the normal third-person game and tested with a gamepad. VR controllers
and the first-person mode are not tested yet on 5.x.

## Changes compared to v0.9.8v2-remastered.2

- **Characters at the edge of the view are animated on 5.x:** the game only animated characters
  inside its own (flat) camera view, so in VR people and animals at the side of the headset's wider
  view stood frozen. They now move normally. Switches in `witcher3vr.ini`:
  `[engine] animated_component_visible` and `async_anim_tick_visible` (both on by default).
- **Recenter key in the launcher:** choose F9, Space or Numpad / (the key used by Luke Ross's
  R.E.A.L. VR mods) next to Camera Follow. A different key set by hand in `witcher3vr.ini`
  (`[openxr] recenter_key`) is kept and shown as "(INI)". The HUD tooltip names the chosen key.

The changes of v0.9.8v2-remastered.2 (FSR per eye, lower CPU load, close cameras, Linux/Proton,
DLSS per eye) are described in its
[release notes](https://github.com/joschi127/witcher3-vr/blob/remaster/docs/releases/v0.9.8v2-remastered.2.md).

## Not included

- **PureDark AFW** (`PDAFWPlugin.dll`), needed only for AER + AFW on 4.04. Copy it from the
  [original release](https://github.com/tig3rmast3r/witcher3-vr/releases) into `bin\x64_dx12`, or
  use a Stereo or Mono mode.
- **Fast Transitions DLC** (optional, 4.04). Also in the original release (`dlc` folder).
- NVIDIA DLSS5 files, as in the original release.

## Installation

1. Extract the archive into the game folder (the one containing `bin`, `content` and `mods`); merge
   and overwrite. When the original v0.9.8v2 was installed on 5.x before, delete
   `mods\modWitcher3VRStateBridge` first.
2. Start `bin\x64_dx12\Witcher3VRLauncher.exe`.
3. Press **Configure Settings for VR** once (also when updating from v0.9.8v2-remastered.1; not
   needed when updating from .2). It backs up your game settings first, and **Restore Original
   Settings** brings them back.
4. Optional on 5.x: tick **FSR per eye (5.x)**, choose the recenter key.
5. Press **Save & Launch**.

## Tips for 5.x

- A setup that plays smoothly at 90 Hz on an AMD RX 7900 XTX with a Pico 4 over Virtual Desktop:
  VR Graphics Quality "Ultra", FOV tangent 80 % horizontal and 65 % vertical, launcher resolution
  AUTO (2292x1884 per eye), **FSR per eye** on.
- `[openxr] hud_controller_locked=1` and `hud_size=0.85` in `witcher3vr.ini` keep the HUD in place
  when you turn your head and make it easier to read.

## Known issues on 5.x

- Distant water can switch between blue and white with the head pose.
- AMD GPUs need Adrenalin 26.9.2 or newer, otherwise some grass renders black (with or without the
  mod).
- The gameplay HUD follows the head by default (see the tip above).
- Windows Defender may flag the unsigned `dxgi.dll` as a false positive.

## Not tested yet

- **Full playthrough on 5.01:** started and still in progress. No blockers so far; later regions and
  quests not tested yet.
- NVIDIA GPUs, including **DLSS per eye** (`[engine] temporal_backend=dlss`; tested by kaarele on
  NVIDIA, not tested yet in this fork).
- Headsets and runtimes other than a Pico 4 over Virtual Desktop (VDXR) on Windows and WiVRn on
  Linux.
- VR controllers and the first-person mode on 5.x.
- Aiming with the crossbow; the cutscene camera light fix in the headset.
