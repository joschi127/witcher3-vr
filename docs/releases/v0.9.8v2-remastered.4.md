# Witcher 3 VR – Remastered fork, v0.9.8v2-remastered.4

Release of this fork of [Witcher 3 VR](https://github.com/tig3rmast3r/witcher3-vr) by tig3rmast3r.
All credit for the mod goes to him; this fork adds support for The Witcher 3 Remastered on top of
his v0.9.8v2 release. Many of the 5.x improvements come from
[kaarele/witcher3-remastered-vr](https://github.com/kaarele/witcher3-remastered-vr) (MIT), taken
over feature by feature.

**On 5.x, use this fork only.** The original v0.9.8v2 is made for 4.04; on 5.x its scripts fail to
compile (`@wrapMethod cannot wrap 'HorseRiding::OnGameCameraPostTick'`) and its game hooks do not
match.

## Game versions

- **The Witcher 3 Remastered, patch 5.01** (GOG): Stereo or Mono with the game's FXAA, **FSR per
  eye** or, on NVIDIA, **DLSS per eye** (launcher choice). TAAU, ray tracing, AER + AFW,
  OptiScaler, ReShade and OFXR are not ported to 5.x yet.
- **The Witcher 3 Next-Gen 4.04**: as in the original v0.9.8v2, plus the fixes of this fork.
- **Newer Remastered patches (for example 5.1c) are not supported yet.** The mod detects them and
  keeps its game hooks off, so it does nothing there. Keep the game on 5.01 for now (GOG: turn off
  auto-update and roll back to 5.01).

The 5.x port is made for the normal third-person game and tested with a gamepad. VR controllers
and the first-person mode are not tested yet on 5.x.

## Changes compared to v0.9.8v2-remastered.3

**Image and stability on 5.x**

- **No left-eye glitch in dialogues:** `[openxr] native_stereo=1` half-enabled the 4.04 asymmetric
  projection, which is not ported to 5.x. In Full VR dialogues the left eye then briefly showed the
  right eye's image now and then. Merely opening the launcher set it to 1 until the next Save. The
  mod now ignores `native_stereo` (and the F2 toggle) on 5.01, and the launcher keeps it at 0 on
  5.x. Headset: glitch in 4 of 4 dialogue runs before, 0 of 3 after.
- **No flash at cutscene camera cuts with FSR or DLSS per eye:** the game never restarts the
  upscaler history at a camera cut, so after a cut in a dialogue or cutscene both eyes briefly
  blended in the previous shot. The mod now restarts both eye histories at every Full VR camera cut
  (`[engine] remaster_cut_history_reset`, on by default), as it already did for TAAU on 4.04.
- **No double image behind the system menu:** the game keeps rendering both eyes behind the system
  menu, and the menu panel alternated between them from frame to frame, so the scene behind the
  menu flickered as a double image. The panel now shows one eye (the background runs at half the
  frame rate there). The menu itself is unchanged.
- **No black rectangle during fades:** the game draws its fade to black (loading, scene changes,
  fast travel) into the HUD layer, which showed as a black HUD-sized rectangle in VR. The whole view
  now darkens with the fade instead (`[openxr] hud_fade_mode=uniform`, default; `remove` drops the
  fade, `native` keeps the game's rectangle). Taken over from kaarele's fork, with `uniform` as the
  default here because `remove` showed a short flash at the start of cutscenes.

**HUD and menus on 5.x**

- **Sharper HUD text:** a HUD made smaller with `hud_size` skipped pixels, so text broke up and
  shimmered with head motion. The HUD is now filtered (`[openxr] hud_filtered_sampling`, on by
  default on 5.x, off on 4.04). Taken over from kaarele's fork.
- **Subtitles stay in place with the controller-locked HUD:** with **Controller-locked HUD**
  (`[openxr] hud_controller_locked=1`) the HUD already stayed in the recentered direction during
  gameplay; now subtitles and dialogue choices in Full VR cutscenes and dialogues do too.
- **Loading screen stays in place:** the main menu and the loading screen after Continue followed
  every head turn, which felt jerky with the loading screen's moving picture. They are now fixed in
  the room like the in-game menus, in the direction you look when they appear (placed again if the
  headset moves more than 0.5 m, for example while putting it on). 4.04 is unchanged.

**Launcher**

- **DLSS per eye (5.x, NVIDIA):** the "FSR per eye (5.x)" checkbox is now an anti-aliasing choice
  next to Integration: **FXAA (5.x)**, **FSR per eye (5.x)** or **DLSS per eye (5.x)**. DLSS per eye
  writes the game's DLSS (`AAMode=6`, `AllowDLSS=true`) and `[engine] temporal_backend=dlss`, which
  before had to be set by hand. Confirmed on an NVIDIA RTX 5090 by a user (#23). The Linux tool
  accepts `configure --aa dlss` on 5.01 as well.

**For testers**

- `[debug] history_event_log=1` writes only the per-eye upscaler history events (resets, camera
  cuts) to `witcher3vr.log`, without the cost of the full diagnostics.

The changes of v0.9.8v2-remastered.3 and .2 are described in their release notes
([.3](https://github.com/joschi127/witcher3-vr/blob/remaster/docs/releases/v0.9.8v2-remastered.3.md),
[.2](https://github.com/joschi127/witcher3-vr/blob/remaster/docs/releases/v0.9.8v2-remastered.2.md)).

## Not included

- **PureDark AFW** (`PDAFWPlugin.dll`), needed only for AER + AFW on 4.04. Copy it from the
  [original release](https://github.com/tig3rmast3r/witcher3-vr/releases) into `bin\x64_dx12`, or
  use a Stereo or Mono mode.
- **Fast Transitions DLC** (optional, 4.04). Also in the original release (`dlc` folder).
- NVIDIA DLSS5 files, as in the original release.

## Installation

1. Extract the archive into the game folder (the one containing `bin`, `content` and `mods`); merge
   and overwrite. When the original v0.9.8v2 was installed on 5.x before, delete
   `mods\modWitcher3VRStateBridge` first.
2. Start `bin\x64_dx12\Witcher3VRLauncher.exe`.
3. Press **Configure Settings for VR** once (also when updating from v0.9.8v2-remastered.1; not
   needed when updating from .2 or .3). It backs up your game settings first, and **Restore
   Original Settings** brings them back.
4. Optional on 5.x: choose the anti-aliasing (**FSR per eye (5.x)**, or **DLSS per eye (5.x)** on
   NVIDIA) and the recenter key.
5. Press **Save & Launch**.

## Tips for 5.x

- A setup that plays smoothly at 90 Hz on an AMD RX 7900 XTX with a Pico 4 over Virtual Desktop:
  VR Graphics Quality "Ultra", FOV tangent 80 % horizontal and 65 % vertical, launcher resolution
  AUTO (2292x1884 per eye), **FSR per eye**.
- **Controller-locked HUD** in the launcher (or `[openxr] hud_controller_locked=1`) and
  `hud_size=0.85` in `witcher3vr.ini` keep the HUD and subtitles in place when you turn your head
  and make them easier to read.

## Known issues on 5.x

- Distant water can switch between blue and white with the head pose.
- AMD GPUs need Adrenalin 26.9.2 or newer, otherwise some grass renders black (with or without the
  mod).
- The gameplay HUD follows the head by default (see the tip above).
- The scene behind the system menu has no anti-aliasing and runs at half the frame rate.
- Windows Defender may flag the unsigned `dxgi.dll` as a false positive.
