# Witcher 3 VR – Remastered fork, v0.9.8v2-remastered.5

Release of this fork of [Witcher 3 VR](https://github.com/tig3rmast3r/witcher3-vr) by tig3rmast3r.
All credit for the mod goes to him; this fork adds support for The Witcher 3 Remastered on top of
his v0.9.8v2 release. Many of the 5.x improvements come from
[kaarele/witcher3-remastered-vr](https://github.com/kaarele/witcher3-remastered-vr) (MIT), taken
over feature by feature.

**On 5.x, use this fork only.** The original v0.9.8v2 is made for 4.04; on 5.x its scripts fail to
compile (`@wrapMethod cannot wrap 'HorseRiding::OnGameCameraPostTick'`) and its game hooks do not
match.

## Game versions

- **The Witcher 3 Remastered, patch 5.01** (GOG): Stereo or Mono with the game's FXAA, **FSR per
  eye** or, on NVIDIA, **DLSS per eye** (launcher choice). TAAU, ray tracing, AER + AFW,
  OptiScaler, ReShade and OFXR are not ported to 5.x yet.
- **The Witcher 3 Next-Gen 4.04**: as in the original v0.9.8v2, plus the fixes of this fork.
- **Newer Remastered patches (for example 5.1c) are not supported yet.** The mod detects them and
  keeps its game hooks off, so it does nothing there. Keep the game on 5.01 for now (GOG: turn off
  auto-update and roll back to 5.01).
- **Outlook (not decided yet):** a future release may drop 4.04 support in this fork and instead
  support the two newest 5.x patches at a time. For 4.04 the
  [original mod](https://github.com/tig3rmast3r/witcher3-vr) remains available.

The 5.x port is made for the normal third-person game and tested with a gamepad. VR controllers
and the first-person mode are not tested yet on 5.x.

## Changes compared to v0.9.8v2-remastered.4

- **System menu at full frame rate and with anti-aliasing (5.x):** the double image behind the
  system menu had a simple cause: the game renders one view behind a menu, but the mod still moved
  the camera by half the eye distance, alternating every frame. That shift is now skipped while a
  menu is open, and FSR/DLSS keep one history for the menu view. The workaround of .4 (every second
  frame held) is removed, so the scene behind the menu runs at the full frame rate and is
  anti-aliased again. Taken over from kaarele's fork (`MENU-MONO-CAMERA`), plus the FSR part.
- **Sun shadows once per eye pair (5.x):** the game computed the sun shadow cascades for each eye
  separately, although both eyes of a pair see the same sun shadows. The second eye now reuses the
  first eye's shadow map (`[engine] remaster_shadow_pair_reuse=1`, default; `0` turns it off). It
  checks that nothing changed in between and switches itself off after repeated mismatches. Saves
  about 3.5 ms of shadow work per eye pair in the test scene. Taken over from kaarele's fork.
- **Controller-locked HUD with DLSS per eye (5.x):** with the game's DLSS the HUD stayed glued to
  the headset although **Controller-locked HUD** was on: the mod could not tell which eye a captured
  HUD image belonged to and kept the game's own, head-locked HUD. It now matches them by submission
  order with DLSS per eye (#23, confirmed by the reporter on an NVIDIA GPU). INI override:
  `[engine] remaster_submitted_hud_join` (1 = always, 0 = never).
- **Docs: recommended setup on 5.x.** The README now recommends Windows with a Quest or Pico over
  Virtual Desktop (symmetric field of view, which the 5.x port renders without cropping) and
  explains the performance tip of reducing the FOV tangent in the Virtual Desktop Streamer settings
  on the PC.

The changes of earlier releases are described in their release notes
([.4](https://github.com/joschi127/witcher3-vr/blob/remaster/docs/releases/v0.9.8v2-remastered.4.md),
[.3](https://github.com/joschi127/witcher3-vr/blob/remaster/docs/releases/v0.9.8v2-remastered.3.md),
[.2](https://github.com/joschi127/witcher3-vr/blob/remaster/docs/releases/v0.9.8v2-remastered.2.md)).

## Not included

- **PureDark AFW** (`PDAFWPlugin.dll`), needed only for AER + AFW on 4.04. Copy it from the
  [original release](https://github.com/tig3rmast3r/witcher3-vr/releases) into `bin\x64_dx12`, or
  use a Stereo or Mono mode.
- **Fast Transitions DLC** (optional, 4.04). Also in the original release (`dlc` folder).
- NVIDIA DLSS5 files, as in the original release.

## Installation

1. Extract the archive into the game folder (the one containing `bin`, `content` and `mods`); merge
   and overwrite. When the original v0.9.8v2 was installed on 5.x before, delete
   `mods\modWitcher3VRStateBridge` first.
2. Start `bin\x64_dx12\Witcher3VRLauncher.exe`.
3. Press **Configure Settings for VR** once (also when updating from v0.9.8v2-remastered.1; not
   needed when updating from .2, .3 or .4). It backs up your game settings first, and **Restore
   Original Settings** brings them back.
4. Optional on 5.x: choose the anti-aliasing (**FSR per eye (5.x)**, or **DLSS per eye (5.x)** on
   NVIDIA) and the recenter key.
5. Press **Save & Launch**.

## Tips for 5.x

- A setup that plays smoothly at 90 Hz on an AMD RX 7900 XTX with a Pico 4 over Virtual Desktop:
  VR Graphics Quality "Ultra", FOV tangent 80 % horizontal and 65 % vertical, launcher resolution
  AUTO (2292x1884 per eye), **FSR per eye**.
- **Controller-locked HUD** in the launcher (or `[openxr] hud_controller_locked=1`) and
  `hud_size=0.85` in `witcher3vr.ini` keep the HUD and subtitles in place when you turn your head
  and make them easier to read.

## Known issues on 5.x

- Distant water can switch between blue and white with the head pose.
- AMD GPUs need Adrenalin 26.9.2 or newer, otherwise some grass renders black (with or without the
  mod).
- The gameplay HUD follows the head by default (see the tip above).
- One user (NVIDIA, Steam Frame over SteamVR) still sees a head-locked HUD with FSR or FXAA and
  **Controller-locked HUD** on; under investigation (#23). Worth a try in that case:
  `[engine] remaster_submitted_hud_join=1` in `witcher3vr.ini`.
- With the controller-locked HUD the crossbow reticle stays in the centre and does not follow
  vertical aiming.
- The scene behind the system menu shimmers slightly and is a little less smooth than in gameplay.
- Windows Defender may flag the unsigned `dxgi.dll` as a false positive.
